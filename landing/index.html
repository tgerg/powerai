How it works

From data to insight
in three steps

No training. No setup. No analyst required. Just upload and ask.

01
📁

Upload your data

Drop in a CSV, Excel file, or JSON export. Or connect directly to your PostgreSQL or MySQL database. PowerAI reads your schema automatically.

02
💬

Ask a question

Type a question in plain English — "show me revenue by region" or "which products are trending up?" — and PowerAI writes the SQL for you.

03
📊

Get your dashboard

Pin results to named dashboards. Resize panels, rename them, and filter across charts with one click. Share with your team.

Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

PowerAI is built for people who need answers fast, not another tool to learn.

🤖

AI-generated SQL

Ask questions in plain English. PowerAI converts them to SQL automatically, with auto-retry if the first attempt fails. The generated query is always visible so you stay in control.

Powered by Llama 3.3
💡

Instant AI insights

The moment you upload a file, PowerAI analyzes your data and surfaces 5 specific, business-relevant observations — trends, outliers, and opportunities you might have missed.

Auto on upload
📈

Smart chart selection

PowerAI picks the right visualization for each query — bar for comparisons, line for trends, pie for proportions, scatter for correlations. No manual configuration needed.

4 chart types
🔗

Live database connections

Connect directly to PostgreSQL or MySQL. Credentials are encrypted at rest. Query live production data without exporting a single file.

PostgreSQL · MySQL
◫

Persistent dashboards

Pin query results to named dashboards. Panels survive refreshes, can be renamed, resized, and reorganized. Create multiple dashboards for different projects or time periods.

Saves automatically
⚡

Cross-filtering

Click any bar, point, or slice on a dashboard chart to filter every other panel simultaneously. Explore your data interactively without writing a single new query.
